# Pepper's ghost pyramids & hologram fans

`?platform=peppers&views=4`

The cheap crowd-pleaser: the avatar on pure black, laid out for reflection
off a 1/2/4-face acrylic pyramid, or for holographic LED fans.

## Setup

1. Open `?platform=peppers&views=4` fullscreen (F11) on a flat tablet/monitor.
2. Place the pyramid point-down at the center of the screen.
3. Each face reflects its view: bottom cell = front, top = back, sides = profiles.

`views=1` for single-mirror rigs (classic stage Pepper's ghost),
`views=2` for two-face wedges.

## Hologram fans

Fans want a single black-background view: use `views=1` and capture the
region, or the volumetric platform's sink output for fans with network SDKs.
The HUD hides automatically in this mode; the avatar keeps talking,
lip-syncing and gesturing — voice input still works (mic stays live).
